# Every Drop Home 💧

A browser game made for a **charity: water** project brief in the Global Career Accelerator. You guide a yellow jerry can past obstacles to carry clean water to villages. Every drop you spill is a drop that doesn't make it home.

▶️ **[Play it here](https://freya466.github.io/every_drop_home/)**

## How to play
- **[Space]** to jump over obstacles
- Bumps and hazards make you lose water, and refill points top it back up
- Avoid pollution, which contaminates your load
- The game gets harder as you go, and how much water you deliver decides which ending you get

## Features
- Water gauge, scoring, and difficulty that increases as you play
- Several endings, depending on how much water you deliver
- End screen showing water delivered, water spilled, your score, and a **donate** button that links to charity: water
- Palette based on charity: water's yellow, plus navy, cream, peach, and terracotta
